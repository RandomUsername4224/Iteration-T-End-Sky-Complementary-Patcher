Complementary Reimagined and Euphoria Patches are independent third-party projects. Their names, code, artwork, credits and licence terms remain with their respective authors. They are not included in this patcher download. Obtain each source pack from its official distribution channel and retain its included notices and licence files.

Before redistributing a patched shader pack, review and follow the applicable licences and redistribution terms for every upstream project included in that pack.
